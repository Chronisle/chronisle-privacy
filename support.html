chronuli.app@gmail.com

To help us investigate an issue, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Chronuli app version, and a brief description of what happened. Please don't send passwords or personal backup files unless specifically necessary and agreed.

Common help topics

Notifications

If completion notifications are missing, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Chronuli. Also review Focus modes and Scheduled Summary settings.

Personalization

Open the expandable menu on the main screen and choose Settings to select or adjust a wallpaper, change brightness or blur, and choose Left or Right under Control Position. To change an island's completion sound, open that island's settings.

Backups and restore

Use the Backup Center in Chronuli to export or import backups. For compatibility with previous versions, backup files continue to use the .chronislebackup extension. You may rename the filename without changing that extension.

Widgets and Live Activities

Widgets are managed from iOS Home Screen widget settings. If content looks outdated, open Chronuli and allow its widgets to refresh.

Privacy

Read our Privacy Policy to learn how Chronuli handles local app data, backups, optional email support, and device features.

Chronuli was previously named Chronisle. Existing backup files remain supported.
