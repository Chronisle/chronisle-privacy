Privacy Policy for Chronisle

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Chronisle ("the App") is developed and provided by the individual developer Achillefs Kalathas ("we", "us", "our"). Chronisle is designed to work primarily on your device. The App does not require an account and does not automatically send your Chronisle content to developer-controlled servers.

1. Data Handled by Chronisle

Chronisle stores the information needed to provide its features locally on your device. Depending on how you use the App, this may include:

  • islands, island settings, positions, colors, icons, emoji, and timer state;
  • focus/session data, activity history, and pending completion records;
  • saved sets and saved templates;
  • names, notes, and other content you enter into the App;
  • custom images you explicitly select for use in Chronisle;
  • preferences and settings; and
  • local backup, restore, and recovery information.

Chronisle does not use this local app content for advertising, profiling, or tracking, and does not automatically transmit it to us.

2. Photos, Notifications, and Device Features

Chronisle uses Apple-provided system features only when needed for functionality you choose to use:

  • Selected photos: when you choose a custom image, Chronisle uses the system photo picker to receive the specific item you select. The App does not request broad access to your entire photo library for this feature.
  • Notifications: Chronisle may request permission to deliver local timer or completion notifications on your device. Notification permission is optional.
  • Widgets and Live Activities: Chronisle may place limited timer, appearance, image-reference, and daily-goal information in its shared App Group container so its own widget and Live Activity extension can display the features you enable.

These features do not cause Chronisle to send your app content to the developer for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

3. Templates and User-Initiated Sharing

Chronisle supports importing and exporting template files (for example, .islandtemplate files) so you can save, reuse, or share templates.

  • Imported template files are stored locally in the App's Template Library.
  • Exported template files are created only when you explicitly choose to export or share them.
  • Standalone exported template files do not include custom user-selected images.
  • Chronisle does not automatically send template files to the developer.

4. Backups, Restore, Import, and Export

Chronisle can create full backup files so you can preserve and restore your app data. A full .chronislebackup file may contain personal content stored in the App, including names, notes, activity history, islands, saved sets, templates, preferences, custom images, and embedded template images.

  • Internal backups: backups created inside Chronisle are stored in app-private local storage on your device.
  • Pre-Restore Safety Backups: before a Restore, Chronisle may automatically create a local safety backup so the previous state can be protected during the restore process.
  • Export: a backup leaves Chronisle only when you explicitly choose Export / Share and select a destination using the iOS share or file interface.
  • Import and Restore: imported backup contents are processed locally by Chronisle. Chronisle does not upload imported or restored backup contents to the developer.

Exported Chronisle backup files can contain sensitive personal content. Chronisle does not add password protection or app-level encryption to exported backup files. You should store and share exported backups only with destinations and people you trust. A destination you choose, such as iCloud Drive, AirDrop, Files, email, or another app, is governed by that provider's or recipient's own privacy and security practices.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Chronisle keeps local data for as long as needed to provide the features you use, unless you delete it through available in-app controls or remove app data through iOS. Retention depends on the type of data:

  • Deleting current content does not automatically remove copies that already exist inside older Chronisle backups. Internal backups can be deleted separately in Backup Center.
  • External backup or template files remain under your control at the destination where you saved or shared them. You must delete those copies from that destination if you no longer want them.
  • To preserve the integrity of saved sets, history, and recovery features, Chronisle may retain older custom-image files locally after an image is replaced or cleared. These retained files are not automatically uploaded to us.
  • Device backups or cloud-storage copies created by iOS, iCloud, Finder, or another provider are controlled by that service and may follow its own retention policies.

Chronisle does not maintain a developer-hosted user account or remote Chronisle database that you need to ask us to delete. For data stored locally in the App, use the available deletion controls or remove the App and its data through iOS as appropriate. For exported files, delete them from the destination where you placed them.

6. Optional Support Email

Chronisle may offer a user-initiated way to contact support by email. Nothing is sent automatically. If you choose to send an email, we receive the information you voluntarily send, which may include your email address, message, attachments, and basic Chronisle version/build information included to help diagnose an issue.

Support correspondence is used only to respond to you, troubleshoot issues, and maintain necessary support records. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us at the email address below, subject to any legal or technical retention requirements. Email transmission and storage may also be subject to the privacy practices of your email provider.

7. Analytics, Advertising, Tracking, and Third-Party SDKs

Chronisle does not include third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not use your Chronisle content for advertising or profiling and do not sell personal data. The App does not use App Tracking Transparency because Chronisle does not track users across other companies' apps or websites.

8. Data Security

Chronisle is designed to minimize external data transfer and to keep normal app content on your device. Internal backup and recovery mechanisms include integrity checks intended to detect incomplete or inconsistent data before a backup or restore is accepted. These integrity checks do not make exported backup files confidential or password-protected.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for choosing appropriate destinations and recipients when you export or share files.

9. Children's Privacy

Chronisle does not require an account and does not automatically collect app content for the developer. If a user contacts support by email, the information voluntarily included in that correspondence is handled as described in Section 6.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Chronisle, our data practices, Apple requirements, or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be available on this page and will show the date it was last updated.
